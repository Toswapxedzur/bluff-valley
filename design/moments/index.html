Table moments

The full-screen animations for big events; play pauses for everyone while one runs. Gameplay moments just blur the table and play over it. Proclamations — tournament calls, cosmetics, personal news — also bring the banner in the logo's tilted steps, with a bar along its foot that drains for the pause. Pick a moment to play it; “Slow” helps to inspect the timing.

  • Gameplay: the table blurs (it never moves), the moment plays over it, the blur lifts.
  • Proclamations: the table dims, the banner opens smoothly from its centre line, the moment plays, the banner closes back to a line. Its material says what it is: logo blue for a call, gold for a champion, charcoal for a knockout, your new metal for an unlock.
